## Report from the night-shift bus driver (wildcard playtest of The Two Voices, murmur.adamwhite.work)

I played Monday morning through the end screen on Saturday at 360×780, DPR 3, touch on. I picked options by tapping and long-pressing, and I left it alone for stretches. From Thursday on I played lazily on purpose, with a random mix of taps, holds and leaving it alone, because that is how I'd play on a break. Screenshots are in `/private/tmp/claude-504/-Users-abdul-code-human-framework/675a9839-8299-4863-91be-017a92a76ff0/scratchpad/pt-wild/`.

**1. First minute.** My daughter said it's about the voice in your head, and the cover says the same thing: one voice is yours, "the other got there first." I got it at once. The alarm is going, the scribbly voice says "Five more," and I tap "Up." That's my life at 5 a.m. too. I understood the joke and the point in about ten seconds, and it felt calm, not like a game shouting at me. I didn't understand the little grey words on the buttons ("she might", "later, maybe", "if you push"). I worked out later that they mean how likely she is to listen.

**2. What worked.**
- **Things come back.** On Tuesday morning it said "She lay awake past midnight. The third coffee." That was Monday's coffee coming back on her. Same with lunch: I made her say "She found it, didn't she?" on Monday, and later it said "Nobody did the impression again that week."
- **Holding a button to insist.** It's easy one-handed, and she answers back: "Fine. Since you insist."
- **The bedtime notebook.** "Mum's neighbour is putting on a new roof. I know far too much about it." and "Mum held my hand under the table like I was nine." Those got me.
- **Leaving it alone.** When I put the phone down, she lived on her own. One evening she rang Mum herself. On Saturday I said nothing and she rang instead of sending the voice note: "They talked until the kettle went cold." It told me she's a person, not a puppet. Best part.
- **The bus home.** "Mum's number is right there. So is everything else." I'm on a bus every night. That one is true.

**3. What failed or confused me.**
- **I missed things.** I left it 30 seconds on Monday and came back to "She left it on read and watched four videos." Left what on read? I never saw a message. On Wednesday I put it down for two minutes and missed a whole birthday dinner. All I got was "'Not really.' Mum put down the knife." I'd want to know what Mum asked.
- **"The pencil had its way."** It shows up every time she ignores me. I never found out what the pencil is. My guess is the other voice, but I'm not sure.
- **Words I didn't say.** I tapped "Call Mum." and it said "'It's ten minutes.' She kept that one." I didn't say that. Same with "Just say it plainly" after I tapped "That was me." I suppose it's the gist, but it felt like being misquoted.
- **She overrode me without saying so.** I tapped "Leave it" to the sister's text and she sent "Wouldn't miss it" anyway. Fine, but I only noticed because I was reading the log.
- **"I'll be too hungry for bowling."** I don't know why she was hungry. If she was fasting, it never said so.
- **The ending.** The week ended Saturday morning after one moment. I expected Sunday. "Two hands, Monday and Saturday: the other one → the other one, you → you" meant nothing to me.

**4. Did my choices matter?** Yes, mostly, and I could feel it through the coffee, the sleep and owning up at work. It's not hard, because there's no losing. Thursday and Friday went bad (alarm and Mum both crossed out) and I couldn't tell if that was me being lazy or just her week. "You pushed me a lot today. I didn't like it." after only two pushes seemed harsh.

**5. Preachy, moralising or religious?** Nothing religious that I noticed. It isn't preachy either. The kind choices aren't always the ones that work: the lap of the yard ended with her asleep at the board in front of Murat. Good.

**6. Bugs and layout problems at 360×780.**
- **Menu button clipped.** On a "Wednesday" screen the ≡ button is pushed past the right edge. See `s017-wed-idle.png`.
- **Clock behind the log.** The top clock runs behind the times in the log. Wednesday's clock read 19:52 while the log showed 21:25 entries (`s017-wed-idle.png`). Monday read 06:48 next to a 06:59 entry (`s003-up.png`).
- **Stray log lines.** A bare "13:10 Feet on the floor." and "21:25 Feet on the floor." appear in the day log with no context (`s017-wed-idle.png`).
- **Duplicate notebook line.** Wednesday's notebook says "I did that one myself. 'Feet on the floor.'" twice (`s018-wed-night.png`).
- **Empty top half.** The upper half of the first screen is blank (`s002-wake.png`).
- **No bedtime choice on Wednesday.** Tapping "on with the day" at 21:25 went straight to the notebook.
- **Strange "TOMORROW" line.** In Wednesday's notebook a "my alarm |" line under TOMORROW looks like a text box, but I couldn't tell if I could tap it (`s018-wed-night.png`).

**7. Verdict.** It's gentle and true, and it's the first phone thing that made me think of my own mum on the bus. I'd open it again tomorrow on my break, mostly to see what she does when I leave her alone. **7/10.**

**8. Three changes I'd make first.**
1. When I come back after leaving it, show a short "while you were away" line saying what was asked, not just what she did.
2. Explain "she might / if you push" and "the pencil" once, in plain words, the first time they appear.
3. Only put words in the margin that I actually tapped, or say "she took it as …". And fix the clock, the clipped menu button and the duplicate notebook line.
